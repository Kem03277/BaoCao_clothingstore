# BRIEF
Self-authored under explicit creative delegation

## 1) Vibe in three to five words
Quiet luxury / modern essentials / city rhythm

References:
- Aesop retail atmosphere
- editorial fashion film with warm neutrals
- old-world tailoring softened by modern silhouettes

## 2) Scroll journey, section by section
1. Recognition — the visitor sees a refined city-day mood and understands the brand instantly.
2. Tension — daily dressing feels busy, chaotic, and under-considered.
3. Turn — a calmer wardrobe gives structure and ease.
4. Substance — premium materials and tailoring explain the value.
5. Range — multiple moods and product stories show flexibility.
6. Commitment — the site resolves into a clear call to shop.

## 3) Energy curve
Recognition = calm confidence
Tension = understated urgency
Turn = relief and clarity
Substance = grounded depth
Range = brighter choice and personal expression
Commitment = decisive buy-in

## 4) How someone should feel, stage by stage
- Recognition: calm and chosen
- Tension: aware of the gap between effort and ease
- Turn: relief as the system becomes simpler
- Substance: trust in quality and craft
- Range: freedom to pick a point of view
- Commitment: confidence to act

Peak: the moment the visitor sees the tailored city jacket and understands the whole brand in one frame.

## 5) One thing this site should do that few do
Make a premium fashion storefront feel like a quiet editorial film instead of a typical product grid.

## 6) Premium-minimal range
Premium-minimal, with warm, tactile, fashion-forward details.

## 7) One unbroken world or separate scenes?
Separate scenes, but with a very continuous visual mood. The page should read as one quiet world expressed through chaptered moments.

## 8) Assets already in use
- Existing storefront brand palette and product inventory
- Fashion photography from Unsplash as temporary editorial references
- No original video footage; stills and layered images create the premium mood in this pass

## Feeling curve
1. Recognition — calm confidence from the opened palette and leading frame
2. Tension — the need for better daily structure becomes obvious
3. Turn — the collection reframes dressing as an easy ritual
4. Substance — material and tailoring explain the elevated promise
5. Range — several stories show a complete wardrobe, not a single hero item
6. Commitment — the final call-to-action resolves the emotional arc

## Peak
The layered hero frame where the city jacket and portrait settle together is the memorable moment, because it turns the brand promise into a visual truth.

## Tell-someone sentence
It is the site where a wardrobe feels like a quiet film: modern, considered, and ready for the city.

## Authored silence
The page intentionally pauses on the editorial calm — no sudden filler loops, no loud motion, and no dead-scroll moments in the closing act.
